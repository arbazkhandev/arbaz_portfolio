About Me

Discover my journey, skills, and what drives me to create exceptional digital experiences

I am Arbaz Khan, a full-stack software developer experienced in engineering and maintaining production-grade web applications, custom ERPs, CRM platforms, inventory engines, and automated business workflows.

My background spans building robust backends with PHP, Laravel, and CodeIgniter 3, architecting relational MySQL database schemas, and engineering dynamic, non-blocking frontends using AJAX and modern JavaScript. I specialize in solving complex operational challenges, ranging from multi-stage automated expiry reminder engines and optical character recognition (OCR) pipelines to third-party payment integrations and high-concurrency database optimizations.

Whether extending large existing enterprise codebases or developing full-scale software from scratch, I focus on data integrity, workflow accuracy, and zero-downtime reliability.
